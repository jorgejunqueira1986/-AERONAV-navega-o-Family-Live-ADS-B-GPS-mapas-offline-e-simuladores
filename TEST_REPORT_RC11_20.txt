Implemented behavior
1. Before takeoff: Family Viewer prefers fresh device GPS.
2. Takeoff is no longer inferred merely from selecting VOO or starting a flight route.
3. With an active flight route, physical takeoff requires GPS groundspeed plus evidence of climb/relative altitude gain, sustained across samples.
   - Designed to cover light aircraft such as C152 as well as airline aircraft.
4. Once TAKEOFF is confirmed:
   - event includes flightPhase=airborne and callsign;
   - Mathia sees "Jorge acabou de descolar";
   - Family Viewer immediately prefers ADS-B for the matching flight/callsign;
   - if ADS-B is not yet available, GPS stays visible until ADS-B appears.
5. In flight, later Journey events carry flightPhase=airborne so a newly opened Family Viewer can recover the handover state.
6. Landing is confirmed only after sustained low groundspeed while previously airborne.
7. Once LANDED is confirmed:
   - event includes flightPhase=ground;
   - Mathia sees "Jorge acabou de aterrar";
   - Family Viewer immediately returns to GPS preference;
   - ADS-B remains available as stale-GPS fallback.
8. TAKEOFF/LANDED events are treated as fundamental Family flight events even if optional Journey timeline sharing is toggled off.
9. Failed TAKEOFF/LANDED event delivery is queued locally and retried when connectivity returns while GPS continues updating.
10. Service-worker notification display is used when notification permission exists, with a fallback to Notification constructor.

Detection guardrails
- Takeoff: active flight route + real device GPS + finite speed; candidate requires >=42 kt with climb/relative altitude gain (or a stronger high-speed+climb condition), sustained for >=3 seconds and >=3 samples.
- Landing: only after flightPhase=airborne; <=35 kt sustained for >=8 seconds and >=4 samples.
- This deliberately delays the landing message slightly to reduce false alerts during approach.
