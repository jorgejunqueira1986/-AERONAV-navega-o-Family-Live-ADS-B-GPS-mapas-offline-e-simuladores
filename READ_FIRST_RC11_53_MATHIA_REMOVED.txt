AERONAV RC11.53 — MATHIA REMOVED COMPLETELY UNTIL NEW QR

On the first online launch after publishing this version, the main AERONAV calls the Railway /family/member/remove endpoint for Mathia using the configured Family code. This revokes her active Family devices and removes Mathia-related location/system rows and stay-history rows from the current Family.

Mathia is also removed immediately from local cards, markers and history. The cleanup retries while offline or if the server is temporarily unavailable.

After the successful cleanup, Mathia stays absent. She can only return after Jorge explicitly generates a new one-time QR/link from “Adicionar Mathia por QR”.
